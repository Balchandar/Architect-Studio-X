# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Architect Studio X a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful. Disagree on technical substance, not on people.
- Assume good intent. Ask for clarification before escalating.
- Keep discussions focused, on-topic, and grounded in the project.
- Credit prior work and reviewers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Harassment, doxxing, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sexualized language or imagery in any project space.
- Sharing other people's private information without consent.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any community channel that uses the project's
name.

## Enforcement

Report violations to the maintainers at `conduct@<project-domain>`.
Reports will be reviewed and responded to within 7 days. Maintainers may
warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban contributors who violate this
code. Maintainers who do not enforce the code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent removal from the project leadership.
